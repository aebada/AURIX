Dear Western Union Enterprise Partnerships team,

I am writing on behalf of **AURIX** to explore a collaboration between Western Union’s cross-border network and **AURIX Gold as a Service**.

**Who we are**  
AURIX is an infrastructure layer for asset-linked digital money — not a custodian. We connect users and licensed partners through APIs (partner reserves, payment network, and governance). Our founder is Prof. Dr. Ahmed Ebada.

**What we are building**  
**Gold as a Service** is a remittance-style flow for physical gold: customers send a gold amount for **pickup or delivery** at jewelry, bullion, and exchange locations across **Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Kuwait, UAE, and Qatar**. We operate a partner store locator, send/track UX, partner applications, and a partner panel for redeem and inventory. Corridors remain in practice / certification mode until licensing and settlement rails are live — we do not claim live cross-border settlement today.

**Why Western Union**  
Your agent and digital ecosystem is the global standard for cash and wallet remittance last-mile. We see complementary opportunity rather than competition:

1. **Co-branded or referral last-mile** — where money transfer and physical gold fulfillment serve the same family corridors in the GCC and Egypt.  
2. **Agent / exchange network adjacency** — exploring whether selected Western Union agent or exchange partners could also act as certified gold pickup points under AURIX rules (vault, insurance, KYC, commission share).  
3. **Product discussion** — how gold remittance pickup might sit alongside remittance-as-a-service / FI partnership models you already offer.

**Clear ask**  
A short intro call (30 minutes) with the right Business Development or Network Partnerships contact for **Middle East & Africa / enterprise partnerships**, to assess fit, compliance gates, and whether a formal exploration (NDA + pilot corridor) makes sense.

Happy to share a one-pager, demo of the send/track and partner flows, and our current prospect footprint (jewelry, bullion, exchange, souk — marked as prospects, not signed agents).

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing who the best person is on your side.
