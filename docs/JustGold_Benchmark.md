# JustGold-style market benchmark (AURIX partner pitch)

**Purpose:** Internal reference for how AURIX frames Gold-as-a-Service partner economics when speaking with jewelry, bullion, and exchange prospects. This is a **market positioning benchmark**, not a product clone.

**Honesty note:** JustGold (and similar regional gold-transfer / retail-gold brands) are independent companies with their own trademarks, UX, compliance posture, and commercial terms. AURIX does **not** copy their IP, brand assets, or proprietary flows. Publicly discussed pricing and onboarding patterns are used only as a **competitive landscape reference** so partner conversations stay realistic.

---

## What “JustGold-style” means in partner talk tracks

| Theme | Market reference (typical framing) | AURIX partner pitch (honest framing) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer FX / spread | Often marketed around a **~3%** all-in feel vs cash FX / informal channels | AURIX quotes **configurable corridor fees** (service + partner flat + FX spread). Practice UI shows breakdown; live rails stay **off until licensed**. Do not promise a fixed 3% for every corridor. |
| Setup fee | “Zero setup fee” / low friction to list a store | **Zero setup fee framing** for prospects: listing as a prospect is free and is **not** a signed commercial agreement. Commercial terms (commission share, SLAs) come after diligence. |
| Onboarding speed | Fast store onboarding narrative (days, not quarters) | **Fast onboarding path**: public locator → application → Partner Panel practice → corridor activation **after** licensing and KYB. Speed is operational readiness, not bypassing compliance. |
| Network | Dense retail pickup / redemption points | Seed + prospect locator across SA / EG / KW / AE / QA; map markers where lat/lng exist; partners appear publicly once approved. |
| Settlement | Live consumer transfers | AURIX keeps **practice / demo** transfers until corridor certification. Never enable live settlement from marketing copy alone. |

---

## Pitch one-liners (safe)

1. **“Comparable economics, not a copy.”** Partners hear familiar ~3%-class customer economics as a *market* benchmark; AURIX still configures fees per corridor and discloses the breakdown.
2. **“Zero setup fee to start the conversation.”** Apply, appear as prospect, train on Partner Panel — commercial close after KYB.
3. **“Fast onboarding, licensed go-live.”** Counter redemption, inventory, and earnings tools ship early; live cross-border settlement waits for rails and licenses.

---

## What not to say

- Do not claim AURIX *is* JustGold, is affiliated with them, or reuses their trademarks.
- Do not guarantee identical spreads, settlement times, or coverage maps.
- Do not imply live settlement is on for any corridor until product flags and legal say so.
