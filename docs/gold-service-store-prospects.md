## Notes

- Contact fields (website, email, phone, address, lat/lng) are filled only from public pages; blanks mean not found or not confidently verified.
- Types: `jewelry`, `bullion`, `exchange`, `souk`, `chain`.
- Prefer chains and well-known dealers; souk rows are district landmarks rather than single shops.
- Re-verify phones and hours before outreach — listings can change.
